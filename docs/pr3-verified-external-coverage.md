# PR3: verified external coverage

Baseline: main c958cc45. Target: independently supported new-company coverage;
80+ remains an uncalibrated official target, not a prediction.

## Scope and ordering

1. Atomic request, byte and paid-attempt reservations, per-host concurrency and
   spacing, single-flight robots, cancellation. One collector owns snapshots,
   verification, planner updates and the supervisor Pipe. I/O threads never send.
2. One conditional permitted discovery provider. Snippets are candidates only;
   credentials, source terms, rate and cost must be declared before activation.
3. Exact legal operator proof, independently audited. Registry URLs are leads.
   Organisation numbers in directories, customer lists or group pages do not
   establish ownership. Facts must independently identify their legal subject.
4. Bounded visible HTML plus existing JSON-LD. Preserve literal source spans,
   original acquisition time, hash, URL, legal subject and dates. No inferred jobs.
5. Paired external evaluation and failure funnel; bounded execution experiments.

## Promotion gates (declared before experiments)

| Gate | Required evidence | Why |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Discovery | More verified legal-company websites on unseen companies | Candidates do not count |
| Content | Independently matched gains in products/services AND jobs/dated activity | Website coverage cannot replace a content family |
| Attribution | No material wrong-company publications, including adversarial group/directory cases | Recall must retain attribution |
| Efficiency | A current fixed route; B new extraction; C B plus discovery, same cohort, budgets and cutoff | Isolate mechanism from extra spending |
| Reliability | Ordered terminal outputs, charged retries/robots/redirects, bounded cancellation, reader round-trip at 100/300/1500 local scenarios | CI alone is insufficient |
| Official boundary | Organizer wire contract, batch/resource settings and credential route | Local scenarios are not official readiness |

Freeze development and disjoint validation identities before extraction tuning.
Independently label positive opportunities; unknown is not negative. Report all
family denominators, gains, losses, percentage points, costs and source failures.
Use exact two-sided McNemar (conditional binomial) for paired company outcomes;
predeclare products/services and the combined jobs/dated-activity family as the
two content outcomes, with Holm adjustment. Five gains and zero losses gives
p=0.0625, not significant at 5%. A 5 percentage-point change detects a mechanism;
it is not an 80-point forecast. Expand an inconclusive validation cohort without
retuning against it. Never derive reference labels from the maker's extraction.

The 1500-company run is a local stress scenario. Four BRREG requests each at
0.25-second spacing need at least 1499.75 seconds of starts, excluding latency
and retries, so the current 900-second local cap cannot support that scenario.
Declare larger local limits or measure fewer requests without lost coverage.
The identity input remains bounded at 2 MiB; evidence-rich state at 128 MiB must
be tested separately. Actual official limits remain unconfirmed.

Exclude PDF finance, broad jobs feeds, general LLM agents, learned planners and
multiple discovery providers. The model-key enquiry was sent separately; a key
does not establish the official evaluator contract or search access.

Do not merge on more facts, candidates, passing CI or concurrency alone. Merge
requires independently supported content gains and stable execution. Keep a
draft while any mandatory evidence is missing, with exact missing receipts.
