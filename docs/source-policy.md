# Source policy

[Configured source policies](../configs/source-policy.json) declare activation,
access and rate limits. Live adapters use frozen registry identity, official
roles/accounts/workplaces, verified company pages and bounded NAV acquisition.
Optional search supplies leads; snippets never become evidence.

Every published fact requires the exact legal subject, permitted retained bytes,
acquisition timestamp, content hash, support locator and relevant period/scope.
Official identity records do not establish website ownership. NAV name matching
is a lead; publication requires the exact official employer bridge. Robots,
HTTPS/DNS/redirect checks and shared request/byte/spend limits apply before fetch.

Literal named-company assertions on a registry-linked host use `registry_linked_html`
scope. This verifies the stated fact separately from website ownership or catalogue offers.
Dated articles may use an AS-free name only with a matching registered host and full legal-name footer; publication and modification dates stay separate.
Structured self-descriptions may use `registry_linked_structured` when the exact legal name and address bind to a visible legal-number footer on the registered host; both spans are retained without inferring website ownership.
Bounded service paragraphs retain their named subject context; dated post headlines retain matching publication evidence and do not establish current roles.

Source independence means independent origins, not multiple copied URLs. Keep
contradictions and explicit unknowns. Incomplete feed windows and source outages
cannot establish absence. Personal credentials, downloaded bodies and company
exports remain outside Git. Third-party code reuse requires a verified licence;
no starter or competitor code is copied into the implementation.
